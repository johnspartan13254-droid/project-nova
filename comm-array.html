In the quiet blue hour before sunrise, the Virgin‑Being wandered into his cliffside kitchen — a place built for wind, sky, and the hum of distant engines. He expected stillness, the kind of silence that settles before the world remembers to wake.

The Virgin‑Being blinked, startled, then laughed — because of course the Tesla‑Being would appear at dawn, unannounced, quietly building something simple and human into the start of the day.

But the morning held more than two innovators. Outside the frosted window, in the snowy Arctic distance, a third presence stirred — the Gamekeeper-0Being, a calm architect of worlds, someone who understood people as deeply as he understood systems. And maybe, someday soon, the Virgin-Being, the Tesla-Being, and the Gamekeeper-Being will share not just a sunrise, but a vision — because the future tends to grow fastest when its architects finally meet.
